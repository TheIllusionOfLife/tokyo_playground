# Shibuya Open World — Logo & Thumbnail Prompts

Image generation prompts for the game. Use with Midjourney, DALL-E, Flux, Ideogram, or similar tools. Multiple prompt ideas per asset type. Try them all.

---

## Style Guidelines (apply to all prompts)

- **Aesthetic**: Shibuya is neon, crowds, energy. Think the scramble crossing at night, giant screens, 109 building, Shibuya Sky observation deck, Hachiko plaza.
- **Mood**: Energetic, playful, urban, welcoming. Party game for kids (9-15).
- **Color palette**: Neon pink, electric blue, golden yellow, cyan. Dark city backgrounds with bright pops.
- **Art style**: Clean cartoon/anime, cel-shaded, Roblox-compatible. Bold outlines, slightly exaggerated.
- **Hachi reference**: White/cream Akita Inu (NOT Shiba Inu). Large dog, broad bear-like face, thick triangular ears, fluffy curled tail. Calm but friendly. See `icon-prompt.md` for full visual specs.
- **DO NOT include**: Torii gates, temples, shrines (not featured in the game). This is urban Shibuya, not traditional Japan.

## 1. Game Icon (512x512)

Must read clearly at 50x50px on mobile. No text (Roblox overlays game name). Bright, saturated.

### Prompt A — Hachi at the Scramble Crossing

```
Game icon, 512x512 square. Cartoon/anime style.

A large white Akita Inu dog (Hachiko) sitting proudly in the middle of Shibuya Scramble Crossing. The famous crosswalk lines radiate out from under him. Behind him, towering buildings covered in glowing neon billboards (pink, cyan, gold). The crossing is alive with tiny colorful characters running in all directions.

Bird's-eye angle looking slightly down at Hachi. Night scene with warm neon glow everywhere. Cel-shaded, bold outlines, vibrant saturated colors. No text.
```

### Prompt B — Hachi on Shibuya Sky

```
Game icon, 512x512 square. Cartoon/anime style.

A white Akita Inu dog standing on the glass observation deck of Shibuya Sky, looking out over the city below. The Tokyo skyline stretches behind him with thousands of tiny neon lights. The sky is a dramatic sunset gradient (orange to deep purple). Wind ruffles his fur.

Shot from behind Hachi, looking over his shoulder at the vast city. Cinematic feel, but cute cartoon style. Bright, warm colors. No text.
```

### Prompt C — Hachi Close-Up with Neon Reflections

```
Game icon, 512x512 square. Cartoon/anime style.

Extreme close-up of a white Akita Inu dog's face filling 80% of the frame. He has a big friendly smile. In his dark eyes, you can see the reflection of Shibuya's neon signs (pink and cyan). Behind him, a bokeh blur of colorful city lights. One ear is slightly tilted.

### Prompt D — Scramble Crossing from Above

Game icon, 512x512 square. Cartoon/anime style.

A Roblox-style character riding a large white Akita Inu dog, dashing through a Shibuya street at night. Motion blur on the neon signs behind them. Golden sparkle trail behind the dog. The street is lined with glowing vending machines and neon billboards. Dynamic diagonal composition.

## 2. Game Thumbnail (1920x1080)

Landscape. Shown on game detail page. Can include text overlay area on right third.

### Prompt A — Scramble Crossing Chaos

```
Game thumbnail, 1920x1080 landscape. Cartoon/anime style.

Shibuya Scramble Crossing at golden hour. Camera at street level, slightly wide angle. The crossing is packed with Roblox-style characters in a chaotic party game scene: one group playing tag (an "oni" chasing others), another group riding animal vehicles (white Akita dog, calico cat, go-kart with neon trail), someone hiding behind a vending machine.

Left side: Shibuya 109 building with its iconic cylinder shape and giant neon screen. Crowds everywhere.
Center: The action. Characters mid-game, dynamic poses, sparkle effects.
Right third: Slightly less busy, warm sky gradient, space for text overlay.

Energetic, fun, urban. Cel-shaded with bold colors. Warm golden light mixing with neon pink and cyan.
```

### Prompt B — Shibuya Night Panorama

```
Game thumbnail, 1920x1080 landscape. Cartoon/anime style.

Wide panoramic view of Shibuya at night from a rooftop. The entire district glows with neon. In the foreground, a group of Roblox characters sit on the rooftop edge, legs dangling, looking out at the city together. One character rides a white Akita Inu dog. Fireworks burst in the sky above Shibuya Sky observatory.

Below, the streets are alive with tiny lights and moving vehicles. Giant billboard screens show colorful ads. The mood is "hanging out with friends in the coolest city in the world."

Warm, inviting, epic scale. Right side has space for text overlay.
```

### Prompt C — Street-Level Action

```
Game thumbnail, 1920x1080 landscape. Cartoon/anime style.

Low-angle shot from street level in a Shibuya back alley. A game of can-kick in progress: one character kicks a shiny can high into the air while others scatter and hide behind trash cans, vending machines, and parked bicycles. A white Akita Inu dog watches from the corner. Neon signs from the main street bleed light into the alley. Overhead, strings of paper lanterns.

Gritty but cute. Urban playground feel. Dynamic action composition. Left side has the action, right side fades into warm neon glow for text overlay.

## 3. Text Logo / Wordmark

For loading screen, marketing, social media. Transparent background for compositing.

### Prompt A — Neon Sign Logo

```
Text logo for "SHIBUYA OPEN WORLD". Designed to look like a real Shibuya neon sign.

"SHIBUYA" in large bold letters, glowing hot pink neon tube style with a soft pink halo. "OPEN WORLD" below in electric cyan neon, slightly smaller. Both lines have that characteristic neon glow with subtle flickering warmth. Thin dark mounting brackets visible behind the letters (like a real sign).

Transparent background. The glow creates its own backdrop. Clean, readable, iconic.
```

### Prompt B — Urban Sticker Logo

```
Text logo for "SHIBUYA OPEN WORLD". Street culture sticker style.

"SHIBUYA" in chunky rounded block letters, white fill with a thick black outline and a colorful drop shadow (pink on left, cyan on right). "OPEN WORLD" below in a clean condensed font, golden yellow. A small white Akita Inu paw print replaces the dot in "i". Subtle halftone dot texture in the shadow.

Transparent background. Designed to look like a vinyl sticker you'd slap on a laptop. Bold, fun, readable at any size.
```

### Prompt C — Scramble Crossing Logo

```
Text logo for "SHIBUYA OPEN WORLD". The text IS the crossing.

"SHIBUYA" in massive bold white letters, laid out on dark asphalt as if painted on the scramble crossing. "OPEN WORLD" in smaller golden-yellow letters below. The white crosswalk lines extend from the letters. Tiny colorful characters walk across the letters. Bird's-eye perspective.

Transparent background outside the "crossing" area. Unique, thematic, instantly recognizable.
```

### Prompt D — Skyline Silhouette Logo

```
Text logo for "SHIBUYA OPEN WORLD".

"SHIBUYA" in bold sans-serif, golden yellow with subtle orange gradient. The bottom edge of the letters transitions into a Shibuya skyline silhouette (109 building, Shibuya Sky observatory, billboard screens). "OPEN WORLD" below in clean white, smaller weight.

A white Akita Inu silhouette sits at the right end of the skyline, tail curled. Transparent background. Elegant but playful.

### Prompt E — Japanese Bilingual Logo

```
Text logo for "SHIBUYA OPEN WORLD" with Japanese subtitle.

Top line: "SHIBUYA" in bold rounded letters, white with neon pink outer glow.

## Upload Checklist

After generating, prepare for Roblox:

| Asset | Size | Format | Roblox Upload |
|-------|------|--------|---------------|
| Game Icon | 512x512 | PNG | Creator Dashboard > Game Settings > Icon |
| Thumbnail | 1920x1080 | PNG | Creator Dashboard > Game Settings > Thumbnails |
| Text Logo | 1024x256 | PNG (transparent) | File > Import in Studio |

For the text logo, after uploading, replace `ICON_GAME_LOGO` in `src/shared/constants.ts` and `GAME_LOGO_ID` in `src/replicated-first/LoadingScreen.client.ts` with the real `rbxassetid://`.
