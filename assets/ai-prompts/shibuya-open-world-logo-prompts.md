# Shibuya Open World — Logo & Thumbnail Prompts

Image generation prompts for the rebranded game. Use with Midjourney, DALL-E, Flux, or similar tools.

---

## Style Guidelines (apply to all prompts below)

- **Aesthetic**: Anime/cartoon hybrid with saturated warm neon colors. Think Shibuya at dusk: pink, orange, cyan neon signs against a deep blue-purple sky.
- **Mood**: Energetic, playful, welcoming. This is a party game for kids (9-15).
- **Color palette**: Warm neons (hot pink, electric orange, golden yellow) with cool accents (cyan, purple). Dark blue-purple backgrounds.
- **Art style**: Clean cel-shaded look compatible with Roblox's aesthetic. Soft shadows, bold outlines, slightly exaggerated proportions.
- **Character reference**: Hachi is an Akita Inu (NOT Shiba Inu): large, white/cream, broad bear-like face, thick triangular ears, fluffy curled tail, dignified expression. See `icon-prompt.md` for detailed Hachi visual specs.

---

## 1. Game Icon (512x512)

Must be readable at 50x50px on mobile. No text (Roblox overlays the game name). Bright, saturated.

### Prompt

```
Game icon for a Roblox party game set in Shibuya, Tokyo. Square 512x512 composition.

Center: A large, friendly white Akita Inu dog (Hachiko) with a broad bear-like face, thick triangular ears, and a fluffy curled tail. The dog is facing the viewer with a calm, warm expression. Slightly stylized in a cute cartoon/anime style with bold outlines and cel-shading.

Background: Iconic Shibuya 109 building silhouette with glowing neon signs in hot pink and electric orange. Night sky gradient from deep purple to dark blue. Scattered neon light bokeh dots in cyan and gold.

Style: Clean cel-shaded anime game art, vibrant saturated colors, Roblox-compatible aesthetic. No text. All Ages appropriate. Bright enough to stand out on a dark home feed.
```

### Variations to try
- Hachi wearing a small golden crown or party hat
- Hachi riding/sitting on a stylized go-kart with neon trail
- Close-up of Hachi's face filling 80% of the frame (maximum readability at small sizes)

## 2. Game Thumbnail (1920x1080)

Landscape composition. Can include text overlay area. Shown on game detail page and search results.

### Prompt

```
Panoramic game thumbnail for "Shibuya Open World", a Roblox party game. 1920x1080 landscape.

Scene: Shibuya Scramble Crossing at dusk, viewed from a slightly elevated angle. The famous crosswalk is filled with small Roblox-style characters running, playing tag, and riding colorful animal vehicles (a white Akita dog, a calico cat, a green dragon, a go-kart with neon trail).

Left third: The iconic Shibuya 109 building with glowing neon signage in hot pink. Tall buildings with animated neon billboards fade into the background.

Center: The scramble crossing action. Characters are mid-game: one group chasing, another hiding behind a vending machine, a dog vehicle jumping through the air with a golden sparkle trail.

Right third: Open area for text overlay. Slightly darker/less busy so "Shibuya Open World" text can be placed here in post-production.

Sky: Dramatic dusk gradient (orange horizon fading to deep purple) with the first stars appearing. A few fireworks bursting in gold and cyan.

Style: Vibrant anime/cartoon, cel-shaded, warm neon glow, energetic composition. Roblox-compatible proportions. All Ages appropriate.
```

### Variations to try
- Daytime version with cherry blossoms and bright blue sky
- Night version with full neon city and fireworks
- Focus on Hachi with riders in a dynamic action pose at center

## 3. Text Logo / Wordmark

For use on the loading screen, marketing materials, and social media.

### Prompt

```
Text logo wordmark for "Shibuya Open World". Gaming logo style.

Text: "SHIBUYA" in large bold letters on top, "OPEN WORLD" in slightly smaller letters below.

Style: Bold rounded sans-serif font with a slight 3D extrusion. Letters have a warm golden-yellow fill with a subtle orange gradient. Thin dark outline around each letter for readability. Small neon glow effect (soft pink/orange) behind the text.

Optional decorative elements: A tiny Torii gate icon integrated into the "I" of SHIBUYA. A small paw print replacing the "O" dot in "WORLD". Subtle cherry blossom petals scattered around.

Background: Transparent (for compositing). Clean vector style.
```

### Variations to try
- Japanese subtitle: "渋谷オープンワールド" below in smaller text
- Horizontal single-line layout: "SHIBUYA OPEN WORLD"
- With Hachi mascot sitting next to the text

## Upload Checklist

After generating, prepare for Roblox:

| Asset | Size | Format | Roblox Use |
|-------|------|--------|------------|
| Game Icon | 512x512 | PNG | Creator Dashboard > Game Settings > Icon |
| Thumbnail | 1920x1080 | PNG | Creator Dashboard > Game Settings > Thumbnails |
| Text Logo | 1024x256 | PNG (transparent) | Upload as Decal > use in loading screen ImageLabel |
