# Screenshot & Video Guide: Tokyo Playground: Shibuya

---

## Screenshot Setup

### Studio Settings
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (16:9) for thumbnails
- Graphics Quality: Level 21 (max)
- Enable: Lighting > LightingStyle = Realistic, PrioritizeLightingQuality = Enabled
- Disable: UI overlays (toggle with Shift+G or hide ScreenGuis)

### Camera Tips
- Use Studio's camera (not Play mode) for static shots
- Hold Right-click + WASD to fly camera
- Shift = slow movement, Scroll = zoom
- For cinematic angles: lower camera height, look upward at buildings

## 5 Recommended Screenshots

### 1. Hero Shot: Shibuya Crossing (Thumbnail #1)
- **Location:** Scramble Crossing from above (elevated angle)
- **Time:** Daytime (bright, colorful)
- **What to show:** Players scattered across the crossing, city buildings in background
- **Camera:** 45-degree downward angle, wide field of view
- **Tip:** Have 4-6 players in frame with different cosmetics

### 2. Hachi Ride Action
- **Location:** City streets, mid-race
- **Time:** Daytime
- **What to show:** Player riding Hachi with items falling from sky, gold bonus items visible
- **Camera:** Low angle behind Hachi, looking forward into the city
- **Tip:** Capture during sky-drop phase for maximum visual impact

### 3. Can Kick: Hide-and-Seek
- **Location:** Back alleys near the can
- **Time:** Evening (warm lighting)
- **What to show:** Hiders sneaking between buildings, the can glowing in center
- **Camera:** Street-level, narrow alley perspective
- **Tip:** Show the tension of hiding with city towering above

### 4. City Exploration / Stamps
- **Location:** Hachiko Square or Miyashita Park area
- **Time:** Daytime
- **What to show:** Player discovering a stamp, NPCs nearby, city landmarks
- **Camera:** Medium shot, player-focused with city backdrop
- **Tip:** Show the Living Shibuya atmosphere (NPCs, micro-events)

### 5. Shop / Cosmetics
- **Location:** Shop UI open with city visible behind
- **Time:** Any
- **What to show:** Trail cosmetics, hat collection, the shop interface
- **Camera:** Over-the-shoulder with shop panel visible
- **Tip:** Equip a flashy trail on the player character

## Video Script (30-second Trailer)

### Structure

| Time | Scene | Audio |
|------|-------|-------|
| 0-3s | Aerial sweep over Shibuya city (real buildings, wide shot) | Upbeat J-pop style BGM starts |
| 3-8s | Quick cuts: Can Kick (hiding, kicking can), player reactions | BGM continues |
| 8-13s | Shibuya Scramble: chaos at crossing, cars, tag action | BGM builds |
| 13-20s | Hachi Ride: mounting Hachi, items falling, evolution flash | BGM peak |
| 20-25s | Exploration: stamps, NPC, shrine, cosmetics | BGM softens |
| 25-28s | Group shot: multiple players with cosmetics at Hachiko statue | BGM resolves |
| 28-30s | Title card: "Tokyo Playground: Shibuya" + "Play Now" | BGM ends |

### Recording Tips
- Use Roblox's built-in screen recorder or OBS
- Record at 1080p60 minimum
- Have multiple accounts or friends for group shots
- Record each scene separately, edit together
- Keep cuts fast (2-3 seconds each) for Roblox audience attention span
